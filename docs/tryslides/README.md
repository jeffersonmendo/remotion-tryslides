# TrySlides project reference

This is the shared reference for the entire TrySlides video project: understanding the product, choosing topics, writing scripts, designing scenes, animating, editing, and reviewing finished videos. It is not only a script-writing guide and it is not engineering documentation.

The shared [audiovisual direction](audiovisual-direction.md) applies to commercials, announcements, demonstrations, explainers, tutorials, and social videos. It defines how the message is represented through voice, music, effects, scenes, and motion, rather than treating screen footage as the default image throughout.

## Required production model

Every new production starts with a 12–20 minute long-form video. Its four 40–60 second vertical shorts/reels, YouTube thumbnail, four covers, and 1080 × 1080 social post are derived deliverables of that same video, not independent short-form or image-only productions. Different editorial treatments do not change this requirement. Follow [deliverables and exports](deliverables-and-exports.md).

## How to use this reference

1. Understand the relevant product feature and its limits before proposing a scene.
2. Select the video's audience, objective, format, and visual direction.
3. Build the script, audiovisual storyboard, and material list together: every meaningful beat needs a visual intention, and every product claim needs real evidence.
4. Keep the individual video package in `resources/<video-slug>/`, confirm plan approval and material readiness before final editing, and use these references throughout production.
5. Record feedback, accepted decisions, and evidence-based review. Propose approved improvements to shared guidance when a video reveals a better approach.
6. Recheck changeable facts and the actual demonstration before publishing.

## Evidence labels

- **Verified product reference:** supported by the reviewed TrySlides repository and user guides.
- **Owner-requested editorial direction:** creative preferences established for this video project, distinct from product capabilities and official brand specifications.
- **Production recommendation:** a suggested convention for this video project, not an existing TrySlides feature or an owner-approved brand standard.
- **Needs confirmation:** do not turn this into a public claim until verified.

Repository review date: **2026-10-17**. Repository evidence is not proof that the same version is deployed publicly. TrySlides is documented as **prelaunch, with no real users**. Do not invent customer stories, adoption numbers, testimonials, or launch status.

Video language is a per-video decision. Keep voiceover, captions, titles, and CTA consistent with that decision. Product names and actual interface labels must remain accurate.

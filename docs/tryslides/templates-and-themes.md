# Templates, layouts, and themes

## Know the difference

- **Template:** a complete presentation to personalize.
- **Layout:** a starting arrangement for one slide.
- **Theme:** coordinated colors, typography, and visual defaults.
- **Presentation format:** the fixed 1920 × 1080, 16:9 slide canvas.
- **Video format:** the frame chosen for a Remotion video. A vertical video does not mean TrySlides supports vertical slides.

## What to show about templates

Preview multiple slides before choosing. Creating from a template produces a separate editable presentation: text, photos, shapes, and drawings are not one flat screenshot. Personalize a title or move an element to demonstrate that fact. Templates are not restricted to Pro.

Each template has its own design foundation. Customization changes the user's copy, not the original catalog. Reset to Default restores that template's foundation.

## Layouts

The three layout recipes are **Blank**, **Split media**, and **Centered overlay**. They are starting compositions, not different aspect ratios, fixed restrictions, or whole-deck templates.

Theme-linked properties update when the theme changes; manually fixed properties stay fixed. Do not stage a demonstration that implies all custom colors are always replaced.

Customization belongs to the current presentation. It does not create an account-wide reusable brand-kit or saved-theme library. Template-specific foundations are not extra choices in the five-theme picker.

This is where Steps become useful. Base is the starting visual state of our slide: the full overview. A Step is another state of that same slide. It lets us focus on part of the explanation without building a separate slide from scratch. Create a Step from the controls above the canvas. In this recording, the new state is called Step 1. It will be our Earth detail. Keep the Earth image that is already in the overview. Move the Sun, the other planet images, and their labels outside the slide area in this Step. Objects outside the black slide are still visible around the editor canvas; do not confuse that editing workspace with the presentation frame. Now enlarge the existing Earth and position it on the right, leaving room for explanation on the left. Do not duplicate it or import another Earth just to make the detail. We want the same element to carry the change of focus. The overview title and question are still present at this intermediate stage; we will change the detail copy next. Switch between the overview and Step 1 to compare their roles. The overview provides context; the detail provides focus. One slide can serve both purposes through its different visual states.

## Delivery direction

Calm, clear, conversational English; useful teacher-to-teacher explanation, not a dramatic trailer or sales read. Neutral, internationally understandable pronunciation is a recommendation, not an approved accent/voice. Start near 130–145 words per minute and prioritize natural phrasing. Never force the original chapter windows by rushing, slow speech or blank padding. A final 12–20 minute tutorial is required; verify actual duration with the selected voice and meaningful planned action/concept holds.

Pronounce TrySlides as “try slides”; Base as the ordinary word; Step 1 as “step one”; Earth normally; HTML as “H T M L”; PDF as “P D F”; TXT as “T X T”; .tryslides as “dot try slides” if reading the extension. Uranus: “YOOR-uh-nus” as a consistent production pronunciation. Read overview/detail as concepts, not file names. UI capitalization is not a spoken instruction. Do not speak punctuation, scene IDs, file paths or editorial cues.

## Voice check before a full take

After script approval and voice/provider selection, use the S01 text for a short voice test. Confirm intelligibility, TrySlides pronunciation, pacing, warmth and lack of exaggerated emphasis before recording/synthesizing all twelve scenes. Do not substitute a low-quality system voice as a final voice without approval. No voice imitation or cloning requested.

## Future clean audio hand-off

Record or synthesize separate files at `audio/S01-voice-v02.wav` through `audio/S12-voice-v02.wav`, relative to the production package. Prefer original PCM WAV, 48 kHz and 24-bit when natively supported; preserve any provider-native original and do not claim resampling creates quality. No music/effects or irreversible denoise/compression baked into the originals. Human takes: quiet room, leave about 3 seconds of room tone at the ends and preserve original takes. Speech mistakes/repetition and accidental long gaps are removed in editing without clipping words, breaths or intentional pauses. Captions/timings derive from the actual edited voice, not the word-count estimates.

Voice and text rights/consent must be recorded before publication. Owner source QA is confirmed in F015; do not ask for duplicate footage QA as a voice prerequisite. Final video plan approval, actual audio production/review, logo/font clearance, final captions and export QA remain separate.

A diagram becomes useful when the viewer knows where to look. Let us read the hierarchy in this overview. The heading names the topic. The shorter question gives the learner a task. The planet labels identify the images, while their order helps us locate Earth. Each piece of text has a different job. Notice the space between the objects and their labels: the words should remain connected to the right planet without competing with the image. Notice how the title, question, images, and labels share the work of explaining the scene. The dark background supports the chosen tone, but the content still needs to be readable. Check the title, question, and labels at a normal viewing size. If anything is hard to read, adjust it before continuing. The goal is not to add more decoration. It is to make the next action clear.

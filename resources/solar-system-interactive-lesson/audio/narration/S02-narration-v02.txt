Before opening the editor, decide what the learner should understand. Our goal is deliberately small: recognize the overview, locate Earth, and explore a focused explanation. We are not trying to teach the entire solar system in one screen. We are designing a starting point for a conversation. The learning question is: where is Earth in our solar system? Our presentation needs three moments: an overview, an Earth detail, and a clear route back. Think of those moments as a path, not a collection of effects. The images are illustrations, not scientific photographs, and this is a simplified diagram. Sizes and distances are not to scale. The layout helps us discuss the planets; it does not model their orbits or real proportions. Keep that distinction clear while you build. Every extra label should help answer the question rather than create another topic to explain.
